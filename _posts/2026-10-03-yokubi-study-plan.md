# Yokubi Study Plan: 16 Weeks to Grammatical Competence

**The guide:** [Yokubi, the Common Grammar Guide](https://yoku.bi/Introduction.html) (64 lessons, Lesson 0 to Lesson 63)
**The pace:** 4 lessons a week, about 45 to 60 minutes a day, 5 days a week. Weekends are for sleeping, tea and pretending you'll catch up.
**The goal:** Finish the whole guide with the grammar actually in your head, rather than merely scrolled past.

---

## Week 0: Read the Instructions (Yes, Really)

The guide says **do not skip** these pages, and it's right to be firm about it.

- [Before you begin](https://yoku.bi/Before-you-begin.html)
- [Preamble](https://yoku.bi/Preamble.html)

Set up a notebook (paper or digital) and an SRS deck (Anki or similar) for example sentences. Total time: about an hour. This is the only week where "I'll skim it" is a bad idea in a different way to all the others.

---

## The Daily Routine

| Day | What to do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Lesson A: read it once, properly | 45 min |
| **Tue** | Lesson B: read it once, properly | 45 min |
| **Wed** | Lesson C: read it once, properly | 45 min |
| **Thu** | Lesson D: read it once, properly | 45 min |
| **Fri** | Review day: redo the week's example sentences, write your own, clear your SRS reviews | 30 to 45 min |
| **Sat/Sun** | Optional immersion: anime, a manga page, a song. Spot this week's grammar in the wild | Whatever you fancy |

**For each lesson:**
1. Read it through without stopping to memorise.
2. Re-read, copying the key example sentences into your notebook.
3. Write **3 original sentences** using the pattern (about *your* life, not the textbook's).
4. Add your best 2 or 3 sentences to your SRS deck.

## Week 17: Buffer and Victory Lap

Every plan needs a week for the bit where real life ate Wednesday.

- Catch up on anything you skipped or only half-understood
- Read the [Closing Words](https://yoku.bi/ClosingWords.html) and the [FAQ](https://yoku.bi/FAQ.html)
- Reread your notebook from Week 1. Notice how embarrassingly simple it now looks. That's progress.
- Next step: start reading real Japanese (graded readers, easy manga, NHK Web Easy) and let the grammar get tested by reality

---

## Rules of Thumb

- **Slip behind? Don't double up.** Just push the schedule back. Two lessons in one day is how grammar goes in one ear and out the other.
- **Heavy lessons get two days.** Lessons 10, 14, 24, 27, 44, 53 and 60 deserve it.
- **Revisit, don't just move on.** If a later lesson refers back to something fuzzy, go back and reread. Everything in this guide builds on what came before.
- **Output beats input.** Writing your own sentences is worth ten extra reads.
- **Ask for help.** The guide has a community Discord ([linked on the intro page](https://yoku.bi/Introduction.html)) if you hit a wall.

*Checkpoints are at Weeks 5, 8 and 12. Feel free to move them if your pace changes.*
